Projects

VoxLab

Unity Asset Store · 2026

A voxel editor inside Unity. Drop any FBX or OBJ model onto the window and it turns into editable colored blocks. Choose the detail with one slider, rotate and rescale it, fix it with simple paint tools, and save a ready prefab for your game.

  • ImportFBX, OBJ + MTL, DAE, BLEND, prefabs and MagicaVoxel .vox
  • EditDraw, erase, paint and pick with undo, in the Scene View
  • ExportPrefab as one mesh, chunks or separate blocks, plus OBJ
  • RuntimeBuild, dig and destroy voxels while the game runs
